# Code of conduct

We want localci to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being
treated badly for it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project or to the tools it uses.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take it the same way.
- Keep to the topic of the project in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, publishing someone else's private information, sexual attention nobody
asked for, and anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

## Where it applies

In every space the project runs: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any place where someone speaks for
the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report
something, use the **Security** tab's **Report a vulnerability** form: it reaches the maintainers privately, and the
report stays between you and them. Say it is a conduct report.
